Delete your Wordsmith account

Last updated 18 October 2026

You can delete your Wordsmith account and its data at any time, even if you no longer have the app. It takes a minute, and it can't be undone.

Wordsmith is a free word game by an independent developer: the Android app com.wordsmithforge.game and the browser version.

Sign in and delete my account

Opens the account deletion screen in the browser version of Wordsmith.

How to delete it

  1. Tap Sign in and delete my account above.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account, or the same email address and password, that you use in the game.
  3. Check what will be deleted, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

In the app: open Settings › Delete account, then confirm with Google or your password.

Can't sign in?

  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and you'll get an email with a link to reset it. Then sign in and delete your account.
  • Can't get into your Google account or email at all? Send us a message with your Wordsmith username, and we'll delete it for you.

What's deleted straight away

  • Your sign-in account, with your email address and the link to your Google account
  • Your username and emblem
  • Your level, XP and the record of XP you earned, your hints, Scrap Bin and stats
  • Your campaign and Master Forge progress, and the record of puzzles you finished
  • Your Daily Commission days and streaks, Daily Stoke, Work Orders, Weekly Trials and Masterwork pieces
  • Your Crucible runs and best run
  • Your friends and friend requests, the nicknames you gave friends and any they gave you, and gifts you sent or received (you disappear from your friends' lists)
  • The list of puzzles you've seen
  • Your notification tokens and daily reminder
  • The players you blocked and anyone's blocks of you, the reports you made and any reports about you
  • The random IDs from puzzles you played before signing in

If you sign in again later, you start again from scratch with a new, empty account.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Co-op and Versus results that other players took part in (scores, word counts and longest words, placings and XP) are kept as part of their history. Your account is removed from them, so nothing in them identifies you: they show “Deleted smith”.
  • Automatic recovery copies of our database are kept by our host, Cloudflare, for up to 30 days. After that, your deleted data is gone from them too.
  • Server logs (such as IP addresses and the addresses asked for) are kept for a few days, at most 7.
  • Chat lines you wrote in a room that's still open stay there until the room closes. Chat is never saved anywhere else.

Data on your phone

Some things, like your settings and puzzle history, are kept only on your phone. When you delete your account in the app, it clears your progress and puzzle history from that phone and signs you out. Settings such as sound and colours stay until you uninstall.

On any other phone where you've played, uninstalling Wordsmith removes what it kept there. In a browser, clear the site's data for play.wordsmith-forge.workers.dev.

More about what we collect is in the privacy policy.

Contact

Need help deleting your account: send us a message with the contact form. Include your Wordsmith username so we can find the account, and your email address so we can tell you when it's done.

We'll never ask for your password.
