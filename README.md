# Wordsmith

A smithing-themed online word puzzle game for Android. You swipe letters on the
anvil to forge words into the mold, then level up and play with friends in
Co-op (up to 4 players) or Versus (up to 5).

**Status:** design phase. See [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) for the proposed
platform, game rules and roadmap.
